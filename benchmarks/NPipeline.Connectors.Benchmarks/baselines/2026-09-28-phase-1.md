# Connector benchmark baseline: 2026-09-28, phase 1

Phase 1 of the connector review, on the same machine and settings as [2026-09-28-e029a2f2](2026-09-28-e029a2f2.md)
(Apple M1 Max, .NET 10.0.12, BenchmarkDotNet 0.15.8, `IterationCount=8, WarmupCount=2, LaunchCount=1`). Only the
connectors whose hot paths phase 1 touched were re-run; CSV and Excel are unchanged from the earlier baseline.

| Benchmark | Before | After | Change | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `HttpBenchmarks.SourcePaged` | 232.3 ms, 210.2 MB | 138.9 ms, 124.2 MB | −40% time, −41% allocated | HTTP-P2: one cached `JsonSerializerOptions` |
| `ParquetBenchmarks.Read` | 745.1 ms, 1,408.5 MB | 696.0 ms, 1,330.3 MB | −7% time, −6% allocated | PQ-6: string columns decoded once |
| `JsonBenchmarks.Write` (Array) | 104.3 ms, 258.8 MB | 100.4 ms, 211.6 MB | −18% allocated | JSON-2: flush every 64 KB |
